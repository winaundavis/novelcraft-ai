# Postmortem

Use after an important task, not after every trivial edit.

Capture only:

- Outcome: success, partial, or failure
- Root cause of important failures or rework
- Verification performed
- Reusable project knowledge
- Whether a recurring pattern deserves a memory update
- Candidate workflow improvement and how it should be tested

Classify entries as fact, observation, or hypothesis. Do not record secrets, full conversations, unverified guesses, or one-off noise. A candidate improvement is not a permanent rule until repeated evidence supports it and the change remains reversible.
